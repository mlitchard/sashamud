❯ the concept of ActingAgent is a design flaw                                   

● The whole concept — not just the EntityKey constructor. ComputationContext
  shouldn't carry _ctxActingAgent either. The Rhine pipeline already knows who
  sent the command via SessionId → GID Agent through acPlayerMap. The actor's

  data ComputationContext = ComputationContext
